M.S. Data Science & Analytics

Advanced statistics, experimentation and data strategy for people who lead analytics work.

Program Overview

Level: Master’s-level program

Duration: 12–18 months part-time

Weekly Commitment: 10–14 hours/week

Credential: Nuvora certificate of completion · not an accredited degree

Terms: 3 terms

What You'll Learn

Core Competency: Data Science & Analytics

Specialization Track: data-science

Capstone Project: Design and analyse an experiment or forecasting system, then present it as an executive decision brief.

Terms of Study

  1. Advanced Statistics – Statistical Inference & Modeling
  2. Experimental Design – A/B Testing & Causal Inference
  3. Data Strategy – Analytics Leadership & Decision Science
  4. Machine Learning – Supervised & Unsupervised Learning
  5. Big Data Technologies – Spark & Distributed Computing
  6. Data Visualization – Advanced Visual Storytelling
  7. Business Intelligence – Dashboard Design & Reporting
  8. Ethics & Privacy – Data Governance & Compliance
  9. Capstone: Data Science & Analytics Capstone Project

Tools & Technologies

Python, R, SQL, Spark, Stan, Looker

Career Paths

Prepares learners for roles such as:

  • Data Scientist
  • Senior Data Scientist
  • Analytics Manager
  • Business Intelligence Manager
  • Data Strategy Consultant
  • Machine Learning Engineer (Analytics Focus)

Admission Requirements

Prerequisites: A bachelor’s degree in any field, SQL and Python or R, Introductory statistics

Note: Coding experience required (Python/R/SQL)

Helpful Background: Experience running analyses for stakeholders

How You'll Learn

Learning Format

Flexible + live: Watch lessons any time, plus two live Jitsi sessions a week (recorded)

Assessment Method

Projects graded by instructors on a four-level mastery rubric with detailed feedback

Support Structure

Instructor office hours, peer collaboration, and career guidance

Tuition & Fees

Application Fee: $60 (one-time)

Tuition per Term: $2,450

Number of Terms: 3–4

Total Program Cost: Approximately $7,350–$9,800

Launch pricing in US dollars, paid one term at a time

Financial planning assistance and scholarship opportunities available
