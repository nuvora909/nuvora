# Nuvora brand kit

The logo is the website's own wordmark: "nuv", a ring with a dot for the "o", then "ra", set in Outfit SemiBold
(600) with -2% letter spacing. The ring uses the Meta-blue gradient (#0064E0 to #0082FB); on dark backgrounds it
uses lighter blues (#4C9DFF to #BFD9FF) with a gold dot (#FFD66B).

All images are rendered from the same shapes and fonts by a script, so they are sharp at their full size.

Profile pictures keep everything inside the middle of the square, so Meta's round crop never cuts the logo.

Rules: keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "n"; do not stretch, recolour, outline or
add effects to the wordmark itself; on photos, use the white version.
