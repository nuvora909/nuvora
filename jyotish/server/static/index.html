HOW THE CHART IS CALCULATED

Show the working.

Chart positions come from astronomical calculations. Interpretations are traditional guidance and remain separate from the calculation itself.

Ephemeris

Nuvora uses NASA JPL DE440s through Skyfield to calculate planetary positions. The displayed accuracy figures compare this implementation with Swiss Ephemeris over a recorded set of test charts; they describe that test set, not a guarantee for every birth chart.

Sidereal zodiac and houses

The default chart uses the Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses. Varga charts, divisional positions, and Vimshottari dasha periods are derived from the same calculated birth chart and the selected calculation settings.

Birth-time uncertainty

The Moon usually moves more slowly than the ascendant. A small birth-time difference can change the ascendant, house placements, divisional charts, or timing details. The Birth-time tab identifies which factors are sensitive for the time entered.

What “maximum difference” means

Arcseconds measure a small angle on the sky. A maximum difference is the largest observed difference in the benchmark set. It is a comparison between two calculation systems, not a statement that all predictions are accurate to that amount.

Interpretation limits

Astrology is reflective guidance, not a scientific forecast or a substitute for medical, legal, financial, or relationship advice. Predictions about health, death, or another person’s choices should not be treated as certain or used to make high-stakes decisions.

YOUR DATA

Privacy, in plain words.

Birth details are sensitive. Here is what this build stores and where it is processed.

Saved charts

Birth details and saved Kundli records are stored in account-namespaced browser storage. You can review and delete individual saved charts from Profile. Signing out does not remove browser-stored charts.

Account and usage

The server stores your account email, a salted password hash, session data, question usage, payment records, Tarot usage count, and saved-chart identifiers used to enforce account limits. Chat sessions are held in server memory and cached answers are stored by account. Tarot questions and draws are not kept as a personal history by this app.

Chart calculations and AI

Chart calculations are processed by this service. For Kundli chat, AI processing is optional: when you check its consent box, relevant chart context and your questions are sent to the configured AI provider. Tarot has a separate per-reading consent box; when selected, the Tarot question and drawn card details, positions, and orientations are sent without birth details. The provider may process that content under its own terms. If consent is left unchecked, the reading uses built-in card meanings without sending the question to an AI provider.

Deletion and consent

Delete individual saved birth charts from Profile, or permanently delete your account there. Account deletion removes account records, saved birth details stored by this browser, and the server's cached answers and active chart sessions. Payment processors may retain transaction data under their own policies. You can change Kundli AI consent when editing a saved chart.

Payments

Checkout stays unavailable until refund terms and a support contact are published. When enabled, digital access appears in Reports after payment is verified. Payment processors receive the information needed to complete and reconcile a purchase.
